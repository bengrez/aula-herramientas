Part 1 · Energy types

What is energy?

Energy is what makes things happen. It is the ability to cause change: to move something, heat it up, light it, or make a sound. You never see energy itself. You notice it by what it does.

The unit: the joule (J)

Energy is measured in joules. One thousand joules is a kilojoule (kJ). Food labels use kJ.

  • ≈ 1 Jlifting an apple up 1 metre
  • ≈ 15 Jlifting a laptop from the floor onto a desk
  • ≈ 40 kJstored in a full phone battery
  • ≈ 300 kJchemical energy in one slice of bread

Spot the energy

Something is happening in each of these. Name what is changing:

  • A kettle boils.
  • A phone screen lights up.
  • A soccer ball flies after a kick.
  • A speaker plays music.
Key ideaWherever something moves, warms up, lights up or makes a sound, energy is being transferred or transformed.
NotasInsistir en que la energía se reconoce por sus efectos. Que ella nombre tres cosas de su pieza que «hacen que algo pase». No detenerse en los números de la escalera: son sólo para dar sentido a J y kJ.

Part 1 · Energy types

Two families of energy

Every energy type belongs to one of two families. Kinetic energy is energy in action: something is moving. Potential energy is stored: it is waiting to be released.

Kinetic family  energy of movement

  • 🏃Kinetic (movement)Anything moving: a car, a ball, running water, wind.
  • 🔥Heat (thermal)Particles jiggling faster. Hotter = more heat energy.
  • 🔊SoundVibrations travelling through air, water or solids.
  • 💡Light (radiant)Light travelling from the Sun, a globe, a screen.
  • ⚡ElectricalCharges moving through a wire.

Potential family  stored energy

  • 🏕Gravitational potentialBecause of height: a diver on a platform, water in a dam.
  • 🏹Elastic potentialStretched or squashed: a bow, a spring, a trampoline.
  • 🔋Chemical potentialIn the bonds of food, fuel, batteries, wood.
  • ☢NuclearInside the nucleus of atoms: the Sun, power stations.
Watch outHeat, light, sound and electricity feel like "things", but in every one of them something is moving: particles, waves or charges. That is why they belong to the kinetic family, not the potential family.
NotasLa confusión típica: calor, luz y sonido parecen «cosas» y no movimiento. En los tres algo se mueve. La profesora puede decir «thermal» en vez de «heat»: es lo mismo.

Part 2 · Energy transformations

Transfer or transformation?

Energy is always on the move. Sometimes it moves to a new place, sometimes it changes into a new type. The test uses both words, so keep them apart.

Energy transfer

Same type, new place. Energy passes from one object to another without changing type.

Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

Part 3 · Energy efficiency

Useful energy and wasted energy

Every device takes energy in and gives energy out. Part of the output does the job you wanted (useful). The rest goes somewhere you did not want (wasted), and it is almost always heat, sometimes sound.

Part 3 · Energy efficiency · Calculator allowed

Efficiency: the formula

efficiency (%) = useful energy out ÷ total energy in × 100and: wasted energy = total energy in − useful energy out

Worked examples

1. A torch takes 200 J from its battery and gives out 30 J of light.

  1. Formula: efficiency = useful ÷ total × 100
  2. Substitute: 30 ÷ 200 × 100
  3. Answer: 15%. Wasted = 200 − 30 = 170 J (as heat)

2. An LED globe is 40% efficient and takes in 50 J. Useful light = 40 ÷ 100 × 50 = 20 J.

3. A motor is 60% efficient and delivers 300 J of useful energy. Energy in = 300 ÷ 0.60 = 500 J.

Test tipWrite the formula, then the numbers, then the answer with its unit (% or J). Each line can earn a mark. Efficiency can never be more than 100%.

Try your own numbers

NotasCon calculadora. Siempre tres líneas: fórmula, sustitución, resultado con unidad. Que resuelva el ejemplo 3 sola antes de mirar. Luego que invente números para la calculadora y prediga el resultado antes de escribirlos.

Part 3 · Energy efficiency · Ruler and pencil

Sankey diagrams

A Sankey diagram is an energy chain drawn to scale: the width of each arrow shows how much energy it carries.

The rules

  1. Energy flows from left to right.
  2. Arrow width is proportional to the energy. Choose a scale, e.g. 1 mm = 10 J.
  3. The input arrow is as wide as all the output arrows added together (conservation of energy).
  4. Useful energy carries straight on. Wasted energy bends away, usually downwards.
  5. Label every arrow with the energy type and the amount (J or %).
  6. Use a ruler and draw in pencil first.

Build one

NotasElegir escala y medir con regla en papel: la prueba pide regla y lápiz mina. Probar 1 mm = 10 J con la ampolleta (500 J in, 100 J luz, 400 J calor) y luego cambiar a 1 mm = 20 J para ver que la forma no cambia, sólo el tamaño. Si los outputs no suman el input, el diagrama muestra el hueco: usarlo para volver a la ley de conservación.

Part 3 · Energy efficiency · Try it

Read the Sankey

1. An old-style globe

What is the efficiency of the globe?

efficiency = useful ÷ total × 100 = 100 ÷ 500 × 100 = 20%. Wasted: 400 J as heat, so 80% of the energy warms the room instead of lighting it.

2. A hair dryer, in percentages

The dryer takes in 800 J. How many joules are useful?

Useful = 85% of 800 J = 0.85 × 800 = 680 J (heat in the air plus moving air). Wasted = 15% of 800 J = 120 J as sound and heat that escapes from the casing.
Reading orderFirst find the input arrow (the widest one, on the left). Then decide which output is useful for the job. Only then pick up the calculator.
NotasLeer el diagrama antes de calcular: ¿cuál es el input? ¿cuál es útil? En el secador el calor ES útil: buen momento para repetir la advertencia de la diapositiva 9.

Summary

Six things to remember

  1. Energy makes things happen. Measured in joules (J); 1000 J = 1 kJ.
  2. Two families. Kinetic = moving (movement, heat, sound, light, electrical). Potential = stored (gravitational, elastic, chemical, nuclear).
  3. Transfer = same type, new place. Transformation = new type. Draw chains with boxes and arrows, and always show the wasted output.
  4. Conservation. Energy cannot be created or destroyed. Wasted energy is not lost: it became heat or sound.
  5. Efficiency = useful ÷ total × 100. Write formula, numbers, answer with unit. Never above 100%.
  6. Sankey diagrams are chains drawn to scale: width = energy, input width = all outputs added, useful straight on, wasted bends down. Ruler, pencil, labels.
